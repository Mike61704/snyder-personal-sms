Snyder Personal SMS

A personal one-to-one messaging service: the owner texts his own self-hosted AI assistant for questions, reminders, schedule summaries, alerts, and notifications. There is no marketing and no bulk messaging.

SMS opt-in & program

Text START or HELLO to (830) 406-4837 to enroll in the program.

By texting START to (830) 406-4837, you consent to receive recurring text (SMS/MMS) messages of reminders, alerts, schedule summaries, and system notifications generated by the Snyder Personal SMS software assistant from this number. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Message frequency varies based on the schedules and triggers you configure. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to cancel all text messages from this program, or HELP for help.
