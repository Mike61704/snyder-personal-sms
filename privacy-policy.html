Privacy Policy

Program: Snyder Personal SMS
Last updated: November 7, 2026

This is a private, one-to-one messaging service between a single individual (the account owner) and his own self-hosted software assistant. It is not a marketing program.

What data is involved

  • The owner's mobile phone number, used solely to send and receive his own messages. By texting the program number he gives explicit consent to receive text (SMS/MMS) messages from this program.
  • The content of the text messages he sends and the replies generated back.
  • Standard carrier and messaging-provider delivery logs (timestamps, delivery status).

How it is used

  • Messages are processed only to answer the owner's requests: reminders, schedule summaries, weather and news alerts, and system notifications for himself.
  • Message processing happens on the owner's own equipment.

Sharing

  • No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties.
  • No data is used for marketing or advertising of any kind.
  • No analytics or tracking cookies are used on this website.

Retention and deletion

  • Message history is retained only as long as the owner keeps the service running, and he may delete it at any time.
  • Texting the word STOP ends messaging for the number, which is also the deletion request path for future message content.
