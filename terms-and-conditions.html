Program: Snyder Personal SMS
Last updated: November 7, 2026

Description

Snyder Personal SMS is a private, person-to-person messaging program in which one individual (the account owner) exchanges text messages with his own self-hosted software assistant. All messages are requested by, addressed to, and consented to by the account owner personally. There is no marketing, no promotions, and no bulk messaging.

Message frequency

Message frequency is entirely user-driven: the owner sends a message when he wants, and the assistant replies to each message, plus optional reminders or alerts he has asked the assistant to send. Expected volume is a handful of messages per day, sent only to the owner's own handset.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply. Messaging frequencies may vary. For an individual plan, message and data rates may apply and may vary depending on the carrier.

Support and opt-out

To stop receiving messages at any time, text the word STOP. To get help at any time, text the word HELP. Reply STOP will unsubscribe the number from all future messages. Reply HELP will send instructions on how to unsubscribe and how to contact support. Supported carriers: AT&T, T-Mobile, Verizon.

Consent

Because the account owner is the sole participant and he personally initiated the service by texting his own number from his own device, explicit prior consent for each message is established by his own opt-in and ongoing use. No other end users participate.
